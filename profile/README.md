## What it does

| | |
|---|---|
| **Content** | Captions and images in your brand voice, drawn from your site |
| **Scheduling** | Posting slots from your own results once there is enough history |
| **Engagement** | Likes and comments in your field, within the daily limits you set |
| **Networks** | Instagram, Facebook, X, LinkedIn, Pinterest, YouTube, TikTok, one dashboard |
| **Control** | Approve each post, or let Pulpi publish on schedule. The queue is yours to read, edit and stop |
| **Security** | Access tokens encrypted at rest; export or delete your data from the settings |

## Three steps

1. **Connect your accounts** through each network's own sign-in screen.
2. **Set your brand voice**: tone, goals, audience.
3. **Review, then let it run.** Step in whenever you want.
